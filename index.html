Work at HBM Nigeria

Innovations Intern, October 2026–present, on-site in Lagos. I designed and built web apps, using AI coding tools (Codex, Antigravity), that digitise three stages of ready-mix concrete logistics: vehicle clearance, dispatch and delivery. The apps are in live use at HBM's ready-mix plants.

The problem

Ready-mix vehicle clearance, dispatch and delivery were running as paper or informal steps. I needed to understand the real process on site before anything could be digitised.

What I did on site

I visited the plants and mapped how the operation actually runs: who does each check, what happens when a truck fails a safety point, and how a load is followed from dispatch to customer handover. I used that map to decide what the apps should do.

What I built

  • A 27-point vehicle inspection workflow that separates critical safety checks from routine defects. A failed safety check requires manager sign-off before clearance in the app.
  • A field inspection terminal where inspectors look up a truck by ID, start an inspection, and view submitted records and the fleet register.
  • A mobile-friendly dispatch web app that captures trip milestones and delays, and a dispatch control tower dashboard that tracks loads from dispatch to customer sign-off.
  • Digital delivery notes that create a record of each handover.

How it is used now

The apps are in live use at HBM's ready-mix plants. I continue to improve them as the teams use them.
